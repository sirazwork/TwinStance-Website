PATIENT FAQs

Helpful answers
before your visit.

Are you accepting new patients?

This is a fictional demonstration clinic. A real clinic would display its verified enrolment status and provide an appropriate intake pathway.

Are services covered by provincial health insurance?

Coverage depends on the service, province and patient eligibility. A production site should clearly distinguish insured and uninsured services using verified clinic information.

Can I book a virtual appointment?

The demo includes virtual-visit selection. Suitability must be assessed by the real clinic, and emergencies should never use online booking.

How is my health information protected?

This demo stores nothing. A live implementation requires secure hosting, restricted access, encryption, appropriate consent and compliance with applicable Canadian privacy laws.
