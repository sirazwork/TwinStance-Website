What you should know.

Are the post counts per platform?

No. Standard includes 10 original designs, Plus 15, Premium 20 and Pro 30 per month. Each design is adapted and cross-posted to one Facebook page and one Instagram profile. For example, Standard provides up to 20 placements from 10 unique designs.

Are ads or advertising spend included?

No. This is an organic content service. Paid campaign management is quoted separately, and you choose your advertising budget and pay the advertising platform directly.

What is outside these packages?

Reels, video production, photography, daily stories, comment replies, direct messages, customer support, influencer outreach and additional platforms are not included. Ask for a custom quote if you need them.

What do I need to provide?

Brand information, accurate business details, approved offers and suitable images or permission to create graphic-led content. Scheduling starts once page access and content approval are complete; approval delays can affect posting dates.

Will this guarantee followers or sales?

No. We focus on consistent, relevant content and reporting. Reach, followers, leads and sales depend on your audience, offer and platform algorithms; specific results are not guaranteed.
