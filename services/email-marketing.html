NexReach isn't just an email tool — it's a complete outreach platform built to help you reach and convert customers across every channel.

📧

Email Marketing

Send targeted email campaigns with full delivery tracking, spam score checking, and smart batch sending to protect your sender reputation.

  • Email allowance depends on your provider plan
  • CSV contact import
  • Batch sending with delays
  • Spam score checker
  • Open & click tracking
💧

Drip Campaigns

Set up automated email sequences that nurture leads on autopilot — define triggers, delays, and conditions while you sleep.

  • Multi-step drip flows
  • Time & action-based triggers
  • Conditional branching
  • Pause, resume & edit live
  • Per-contact journey tracking
📱

SMS Marketing

Reach customers instantly in their pocket. SMS has 5× higher open rates than email — perfect for promotions and appointment reminders.

  • Bulk SMS campaigns
  • Personalisation tokens
  • Delivery reports
  • Opt-out management
  • Scheduled sending
🤖

AI Writer

Write compelling cold emails, subject lines, follow-ups, and SMS copy using the built-in AI writer. Beat writer's block instantly.

  • Email body generator
  • Subject line ideas
  • Tone & rewrite tool
  • SMS copy generator
  • Follow-up sequence writer
📊

Full Analytics

See exactly how every campaign performs across every channel — sends, opens, clicks, bounces, and SMS delivery in one clean dashboard.

  • Per-campaign reports
  • Open & click rates
  • Bounce & unsubscribe stats
  • SMS delivery reports
  • Exportable data
👥

Contact Management

Import, tag, segment, and manage all your contacts in one place — with full unsubscribe and duplicate detection built in.

  • CSV bulk import
  • Tag-based segmentation
  • Unsubscribe management
  • Duplicate detection
  • Search & filter
