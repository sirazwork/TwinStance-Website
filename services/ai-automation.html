Frequently asked

Good questions make better systems.

Automation should be understandable to the people who rely on it every day.

Do we need to replace our current CRM?
Usually not. We start with your existing tools and look for useful connections before recommending a replacement.
Can AI send messages automatically?
It can, when the workflow and risk level make that appropriate. For sensitive or important communication, we can keep a person in the approval loop.
What businesses benefit from AI automation?
Service businesses, agencies, clinics, brokerages, contractors and growing teams often benefit when leads, requests and follow-ups are handled across several tools.
How long does an automation project take?
A focused workflow can be planned quickly; timing depends on the number of systems, permissions, data quality and testing required.
Do you guarantee a specific AI result?
No. We define measurable workflow goals, test with representative examples and report honestly on what improves.

Have a process that keeps repeating?

Tell us what your team is doing manually. We’ll help identify a practical first automation and the next step to build it safely.
